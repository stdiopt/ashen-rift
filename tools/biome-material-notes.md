# Biome floors
Built-in image generation produced the albedo artwork. Technical channels are generated by build-biome-material.py using a material-aware height approximation, periodic gradients for tangent-space normals, and roughness based on moss, cracks or frost. Height also drives slight vertex displacement; physics keeps a flat walking plane.

Generation prompts:
- Jungle: seamless square PBR albedo, straight overhead, damp dark earth, dense irregular moss, scattered small pebbles and thin roots; muted olive/brown, flat diffuse lighting, no perspective, shadows, highlights, objects, text or borders.
- Hell: seamless square PBR albedo, overhead fractured charcoal basalt and rust-red recessed cracks with sparse ash grit; neutral lighting, no perspective, architecture, shadows, highlights, text, borders or large lava pools.
- Frozen Crypt: seamless square PBR albedo, overhead irregular slate slabs coated in pale blue ice, branching ice fractures, white frost crystals in recessed seams and snow patches; silver gray/glacial blue, diffuse lighting, no directional shadows, highlights, perspective, objects, text or borders.

Final assets: public/textures/{jungle,hell,frozen}-{albedo,height,normal,roughness}-v1.{webp,png}. Each channel is 512 square. Castle retains its existing 1024 square flagstone-v2 channels. Materials are cached per theme and loaded on first use.

## Jungle and Hell wall materials
Built-in generated wall artwork is integrated as four aligned channels under public/textures/jungle-wall-{albedo,height,normal,roughness}-v1 and hell-wall-{albedo,height,normal,roughness}-v1. Albedo uses WebP, other channels PNG. Height gradients use a 160-world-unit UV span for walls. Height is retained as a source channel for normals; walls keep zero vertex displacement to preserve geometry and collision boundaries.

Wall prompts:
- Jungle: seamless square PBR albedo, weathered dark brown bark, interwoven woody roots, sparse hanging vines and moss, vertical grain; straight-on orthographic, diffuse neutral lighting, no shadows, highlights, perspective, scenery, text or borders.
- Hell: seamless square PBR albedo, jagged charcoal volcanic cave rock, vertical basalt fractures and sharp mineral outcrops, rusty red fissures and ash; straight-on orthographic, neutral diffuse lighting, no shadows, highlights, perspective, lava scene, architecture, text or borders; suitable for cave walls and spikes.
