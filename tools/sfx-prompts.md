# ElevenLabs sound-effect prompts

Generate isolated game sound effects: no music, speech, ambient background or long silence. Keep the attack clear and the tail short. Each numbered entry is a separate generation request.

1. **Arcane Bolt — 0.3 seconds:** Small magical energy shot, crisp airy snap with a bright crystalline pulse and a short sparkling tail. Light and restrained enough to repeat several times per second.
2. **Frost Nova — 0.8 seconds:** Rapid radial burst of ice magic, sharp crystalline crack followed by a cold rushing wave and tiny ice shards settling. Powerful but compact.
3. **Blink — 0.4 seconds:** Quick magical teleport, soft suction into a tight shimmering whoosh, ending in a clean ethereal snap.
4. **Ice Lance — 0.4 seconds:** A narrow ice spear launched at high speed, cutting frozen hiss with a precise glassy crack and a very short tail.
5. **Chain Lightning — 0.6 seconds:** Three fast electrical arcs jumping between targets, crisp crackling zaps with increasing pitch, ending abruptly without a heavy explosion.
6. **Poison Cloud — 0.8 seconds:** Magical toxic cloud opening with a soft wet pop, followed by bubbling vapor and a restrained sinister hiss.
7. **Blizzard loop — 2 seconds:** Seamlessly looping localized icy storm, soft rushing wind and fine crystalline ice particles. No thunder, no dramatic opening or ending; quiet enough beneath battle sounds.
8. **Arcane Shield — 0.6 seconds:** Protective magical barrier forming around a mage, a rising resonant crystal tone and a soft rounded energy pulse. Warm and reassuring rather than explosive.
9. **Healing potion — 0.7 seconds:** Brief cork pop and tiny liquid gulp, immediately followed by a soft restorative magical chime. No human words.
10. **Loot pickup — 0.25 seconds:** One clean, satisfying fantasy inventory pickup chime, bright metallic crystal ping with a tiny shimmering tail.
11. **Enemy melee impact — 0.25 seconds:** Compact fantasy combat hit against cloth armor, a dry thud with a faint leather snap. No voice or gore.
12. **Healer enemy cast — 0.8 seconds:** Quiet ritual healing magic, gentle ascending green-energy shimmer and two soft bell-like pulses. Distinct from attack spells.

Existing uploads cover Fireball cast, fire/meteor explosion, footsteps, heal voice and four mage damage voices. The highest-priority additions are Arcane Bolt, Frost Nova, Blink, and Healing potion.
