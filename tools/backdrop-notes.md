# Rift backdrops

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each theme uses one original image, copied unchanged into `public/textures/`. Three.js draws the selected image as the scene background, crops it to the viewport, and applies a bounded UV offset based on the character's world position. No extra geometry, collision, lights or normal/height maps are needed for these distant backdrops; the dungeon floor retains its existing PBR channels.

## castle

Asset: `public/textures/castle-backdrop-v1.png`

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single-image parallax background behind a Three.js fantasy dungeon. Ruined gothic castle towers and deep courtyards, distant arches, slate blue mist, dim amber windows. Atmospheric painterly stylized 3D environment, wide aerial perspective looking down diagonally, no visible sky or horizontal horizon. Dark low contrast background with distant environment detail at the edges and plenty of shadowed mist in the center so playable dungeon remains readable. Rich restrained color, subtle depth, not a floor tile texture. Entire frame is the environment. No characters, no monsters, no UI, no writing, no borders. Landscape composition.
